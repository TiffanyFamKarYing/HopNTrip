Unforgettable Moments with Our Travelers

Mount Kinabalu

Our travellers challenged themselves to climb Mount Kinabalu, and the reward was a stunning sunrise above the clouds. Laughter, teamwork, and shared triumphs made this climb a memory our travelers will never forget.

Semporna Islands

Smiles were everywhere as we snorkeled, swam, and island-hopped with our amazing group in Semporna. With turquoise waters and colorful corals, the joy of discovery was shared by everyone – from solo travelers to families.

River Safari

Our customers enjoyed a peaceful river cruise while spotting proboscis monkeys in the wild. As the sun set, laughter filled the boat and camera flashes caught fireflies lighting up the mangrove trees. A magical evening with great company.

Sandakan Wildlife Sanctuary

We shared heartfelt moments with customers while watching young orangutans in Sandakan. Educational, emotional, and uplifting — this experience deepened everyone’s appreciation for Borneo’s wildlife and conservation efforts.

Diving at Mabul Island

Whether it was someone’s first dive or fiftieth, our guests were amazed by Mabul’s underwater beauty. We laughed, learned, and explored together, making each dive a shared adventure full of excitement and wonder.

Kundasang Highlands

In Kundasang, we slowed down and breathed in the cool mountain air with our lovely travelers. From strawberry picking to dairy farm visits, every smile and group photo captured the joy of being close to nature – together.
